---
name: Curriculum question quality
description: Why weekly curriculum questions must be explicitly authored rather than generated from a single fact.
---

For curriculum expansion, author distinct, self-contained problems and quick checks for each lesson. Do not turn one fact into several generic prompts or refer to examples and evidence that are not included in the question.

**Why:** Structural validation can pass while generic fact-restatement templates reveal answers, provide implausible distractors, and fail to assess mastery.

**How to apply:** Require real calculations, included reading passages, concrete recall or application, plausible same-domain distractors, answer-leak checks, and focused review of sampled lessons across the year.
