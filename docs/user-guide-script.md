## Final narration

### 01 Welcome and profiles
Hi! Welcome to Jowb Yaaba Academy. That is Diop Yaba, pronounced Jowb Yaaba. Choose Seydina for fifth grade, or Cheikh for rising seventh grade. This is a friendly synthetic guide voice, not a real child.

### 02 Start a subject quest
Pick a subject quest from the course area. Open a quest card to see its mission, time, and the work you will make.

### 03 Mission and task
Read the mission first, then read the task. Aim to finish one visible piece of work before moving on.

### 04 Narration and transcript
Quest cards have browser narration controls. Press play to hear the mission, stop to pause it, or replay it. Open the transcript when you want the words on screen. Your browser supplies this narration.

### 05 Optional learning links
Some quest cards have a Watch or Learn link. It opens an optional outside lesson video or resource. You need the internet for that link, but the app lessons and saved work can still be used offline.

### 06 Quiz choices
Quests and weekly homework use quick checks. Try a multiple-choice question, then try a true or false question. Some activities ask you to match terms with clues.

### 07 Feedback and retry
Check your answers to get feedback. A wrong try is okay. When retry is offered, try again. Weekly quick checks allow a limited number of attempts, then show a final combined score.

### 08 Quest complete and rewards
When your quest work and quiz are ready, mark the quest done. You earn XP, your day streak can grow, and awards appear as you make progress.

### 09 Progress sheet
Open the progress button near the top to read your progress sheet. It shows completion bars, quiz scores, XP, streaks, and awards for the chosen grade.

### 10 Report and adult reset
You can download a plain-text progress report. Reset progress clears XP, streaks, scores, and completed quests, so treat reset as an adult-assisted action.

### 11 Focus timer
The Focus Mode card is a twenty-minute sprint. Press Start when you are ready, and Reset to return to twenty minutes. Pick one quest, then take a movement break when the timer ends.

### 12 Weekly week and subject
Weekly Homework has 36 school-year weeks. Choose a week, then use All or a subject tab to filter maths, ELA, science, or social studies.

### 13 Daily assignment
Open a day card to read its mission. Use its Watch or Learn link if you like, complete the quick check, retry when the app offers it, and mark the day done after the required final score.

### 14 Worksheet printing
Use Print This Week’s Worksheet to open a student worksheet. It is designed for pencil-and-paper work and can be printed or saved as a PDF.

### 15 Answer keys and parent check
Answer keys and answer-inclusive printing reveal answers. They are clearly labelled for parents or educators. The newly merged parent check protects these answer-revealing actions: ask an adult before showing or printing answers.

### 16 Parent dashboard
Adults can open Parent Dashboard to see both profiles at a glance: XP, quests, streaks, quiz averages, awards, and weekly activity. The interface distinguishes this area, but it does not claim a separate login.

### 17 Leaderboard
Leaderboard shows overall standings, this week’s XP, weekly days, and quiz averages. It is a progress view, not a judgement.

### 18 Offline use
Saved app pages, lessons, quizzes, progress, worksheets, and the focus timer remain useful offline after loading. Outside video and learning links need internet access.

### 19 Install the app
To install, open your browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen or Install. The exact words vary by browser and device.

### 20 Reopen this guide
You can reopen this guide later from the permanent How to use this app control near the top of the landing page. Choose a chapter, then keep exploring. You’ve got this!
