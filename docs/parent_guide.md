# Parent Guide: How to Run the Course

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: Math Quest Arena
- Tuesday: ELA Skills Lab or Reading Adventure Guild
- Wednesday: Life Science Lab
- Thursday: World Explorer Ops
- Friday: Catch-up, favorite quest, or parent-child explanation session

## Keep it immersive without overwhelming him
1. Start with the hero image and let him choose a subject portal.
2. Use one 20-minute focus sprint only.
3. Require one concrete artifact after every quest.
4. Award XP only after he explains the work in his own words.
5. If attention scatters, reduce the task to the next visible action: write one equation, draw one box, read one page, answer one question.

## Movement breaks
After every sprint, use a 3-minute break: wall pushups, staircase walk, ball toss, or stretching.

## Parent prompts
- “What is the mission?”
- “What is the first tiny step?”
- “Show me evidence, not just the answer.”
- “Teach it back to me like I am new to the topic.”

## Completion target
Aim for 20 completed quests or 500 XP before school starts.
