Audio Narration

Use the narration controls on each quest card. The voice uses the browser's built-in speech engine, so no extra app is required. Internet is only needed for optional video links.

Parent Coach Script

Before

“Pick your quest. What will finished look like?”

During

“Show me your next tiny step. Do not solve the whole thing in your head.”

After

“Tell me one thing that got easier and one thing to retry tomorrow.”
