The Problem

Our Answer

Game-style learning, built for visual kids

  • Quests, not worksheets — anime-styled missions per subject.
  • XP, badges, streaks & confetti keep kids coming back.
  • 20-minute sprints with a visible focus timer.
  • Narration reads the mission aloud for low-friction starts.
  • Interactive quizzes give instant feedback.
  • Two learners today: Cheikh (rising 7th) & Seydina (5th).
  • One grade-switcher covers both.
  • Everything is self-contained — no build step, no ads.
  • Progress saves automatically across devices.

Curriculum

Aligned to Ohio Learning Standards

  • Every weekly subject carries a standard code (e.g. 7.NS.1, RL.7.4).
  • Concept → sprint → problem set → quick-check, all mapped to a standard.
  • Four core subjects: Math, ELA, Science, Social Studies.

Why we author our own worksheets

Paid worksheet sites restrict redistribution. Authoring our own content keeps the product legally clean, self-contained, and fully alignable — while we link out to free explainers (Khan Academy, CK-12, Nat Geo) for instruction.

For Parents & Coaches

Honest visibility, zero hovering

  • See both children on one dashboard.

  • Weekly activity rollups and earned awards.

Reliable by design

Progress is saved to a database with a merge-on-write sync, so multi-device use never erases work. A local copy keeps things running even offline.
