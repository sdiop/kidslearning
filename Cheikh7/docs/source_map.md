# Source Map

This course was built from the resource categories and topics in the Cobb County rising 7th grade summer packet and supplemented with reputable visual/video-friendly learning links.

## Core packet topics used
- ELA: 7th Grade ELA Skills Review, 7th ELA Summer Packet, 7th Grade Figurative Language Packet.
- Math: Advanced and On Level 7th Grade Math, Math 7-8.
- Reading: Rising 7th Grade Summer Reading Guide.
- Science: CK-12 Middle School Life Science 2.0, section 1.8, Characteristics of Life.
- Social Studies: Latitude and Longitude, Factors that Affect Climate, SW Asia Economics, Why Was Israel Created as a Jewish State, World Studies chapters/puzzles.

## External learning links included in the app
- Khan Academy: Get Ready for 7th Grade Math and 7th Grade Math pathways.
- CK-12: Characteristics of Life, Middle School Life Science.
- National Geographic Education: MapMaker Latitude and Longitude.
- YouTube search links are used only as discovery links for parent-supervised video selection.

## Notes
The app does not copy full worksheets from the packet. It translates the packet topics into short interactive quests, quizzes, and project artifacts.
