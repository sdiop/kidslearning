# Source Map

This course is aligned to common 5th grade readiness areas:
- Khan Academy 5th grade math scope: decimal place value, fractions, multi-digit multiplication/division, volume, coordinate plane, algebraic thinking, measurement, data, and shapes.
- Common Core Grade 5 ELA: quoting accurately, theme, character comparison, figurative language, point of view, context clues, grammar, punctuation, and sentence style.
- NGSS Grade 5 Science: matter, gravity, energy in food, plants, ecosystems, Earth systems, water distribution, stars/shadows, and resource protection.
- Typical Grade 5 Social Studies: history, civics, geography, economics, culture, maps, timelines, primary sources, and evidence-based inquiry.
