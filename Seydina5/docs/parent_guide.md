# Parent Guide

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: Math Mastery Arena
- Tuesday: Reading and ELA Guild
- Wednesday: Science Lab Quest
- Thursday: Writing Studio or Social Studies Explorer
- Friday: Catch-up and progress report

## Coaching cues
- “What is the mission?”
- “What is the next tiny step?”
- “Show your evidence.”
- “Teach it back to me in one minute.”

## Attention support
Use one 20-minute sprint, one artifact, and one movement break. Keep only one tab open unless the quest asks for a video or practice link.
